Use case: stylized-concept.
Design an ORIGINAL important mechanical character called Golden Stamp for the same mature Japanese mecha novel, with its independent GOLDEN HAMMER and independent tool-transport assembly. This is a new body proposal: Golden Stamp is an autonomous adult machine ally, not a human, not a tiny AI terminal, NOT the hammer itself, and NOT a repaint of the partner hero.

REFERENCE ROLES: Image1 (approved orange/white lion-maned Yongquan) supplies the partner's visual family, orange engineering accents, white armor and expressive mature heroic face. Do NOT copy its long lion mane, orange helmet horns, exact color layout or cyan wrist rings. Images2-3 (the tall red giant and navy/ivory giant) are the PRIMARY mature rendering and physique references: deep recessed joints, large armored planes, long legs, proper engineered feet, convincing dimensional cel illustration, restrained real wear. No previous redesign sheet is used.

COMPOSITION: a premium landscape 3:2 character-and-tool concept board on warm off-white paper. The complete Golden Stamp robot occupies the LEFT 60% almost full height, powerful calm three-quarter frontal stance, one sturdy hand resting on a closed tool-case and the other open slightly as if saying to stop and release the load. Tall adult 8-head mechanical hero proportions, heavy but LONG legs, large solid feet, no dwarf, no cute face. On the RIGHT the same independent hammer system is shown in TWO states: upper-right complete assembled heavy golden hammer stored upright in a support rack; lower-right the very same head halves and segmented shaft compactly stowed in one tracked lowbed tool carrier. The character remains a complete standing body with two arms and two legs in both equipment states; it never becomes the handle or vanishes into the hammer. No other characters, no duplicate full-size body, no robots emerging from the truck. No words, numbers, logos, text, arrows, borders or watermarks.

GOLDEN STAMP BODY: authoritative dependable veteran construction-and-rescue hero, broad compact closed gold-bronze chest with an ORIGINAL recessed square approval-seal medallion with no letters, dark charcoal inset that resembles a thick forged stamping die. Strong sophisticated helmet with wide swept dark steel brow, muted gold crown, two narrow amber eyes, clean ivory lower faceplate and sturdy cheek armor, conveying decisive quiet dignity rather than a grin. Broad gold/ivory pauldrons with deep heavy shoulder bearings, slightly flared forearm braces, five-finger dark steel hands with broad gold knuckles; real shoulder/back load-bearing sockets for stabilizing a shared hammer support frame. Gold and warm ivory major plates, charcoal/naval joints, very restrained orange latch accents connecting the partner's family. Deep short dark waist between armored chest and long ivory/gold thighs and shins; large forged boots. Distinct from lion hero through squared disciplined forms, little ornament and a square chest motif; do not create a hammerhead face or office rubber stamp body. A FEW worn edges and heat marks from working machinery, never rusty junk. One compact waist tool-case, no huge weapon backpack, no loose dangling cables.

GOLDEN HAMMER: a colossal separate forged mechanical demolition hammer with a double-sided blocky GOLD head, beveled impact faces, charcoal center joint and a heavy two-piece axial locking seam. The head is clearly made from TWO matching golden half-head blocks that slide together around a dark central yoke, no cartoon mallet balloon shape. Massive thick handle is assembled from several steel/ivory sections with gold locking collars; a positive grip and proximal brake collar allow the partner to swing while Golden Stamp controls the load/release. The UPPER-RIGHT assembled hammer stands in a low heavy independent support cradle, entire handle and head visible; important tool proportions and striking faces clearly read.
LOWER-RIGHT: the SAME two gold half-heads secured on the wide tracked transporter with the SAME segmented handle stored horizontally alongside/under them. Short stout lift arms and a mechanically braked coupling ring are visible but folded. The carrier is equipment transportation, not another sentient mech. Both equipment views must conserve the head's geometry, colors, locking seams and handle sections. Body size need not be precisely scaled to sheet insets; treat the separate tool views as detailed product sketches of the same system.

ART DIRECTION: sophisticated dramatic Japanese mecha hand illustration, very precise dark blue-black variable-weight lines, deep occlusion under overlapping thick armor, hard cel-painted shadow planes with subtle surface depth and sparse purposeful scratches. Warm brass gold is matte industrial plated metal with controlled highlights, not glitter, chrome or neon. Major body masses and handsome coherent silhouette come before rivets. No cartoon cuteness, stumpy legs, PBR plastic toy, exposed scaffold body, dense meaningless greebles or tiny detail grids.
