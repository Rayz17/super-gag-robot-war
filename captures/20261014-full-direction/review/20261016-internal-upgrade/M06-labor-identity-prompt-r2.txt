Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: original mecha design exploration sheet for the Chinese game 超GAG机器人大战.
Primary request: substantially redesign M06 预算二号救援机 into a memorable, competent, low-budget municipal repair and emergency POWER-SUPPLY Labor. This is a new proposal, not an approved design. Derive its whole physical language from civilian working machinery and field repair, the occupational side of Patlabor, without reproducing a named Patlabor mecha. The robot is a lovable veteran public worker, not a comic junk pile, soldier or knight.

Input images:
Image 1 is the previous M06 proposal. Keep only its established yellow / teal / warm ivory / graphite palette, yellow gloved manipulators and the general adult sturdy standing stature. Its generic armor and round soldier helmet are the problem: replace them. Keep physical LEFT shin as the single teal replacement part (viewer-right in front view), right shin predominantly yellow. LEFT hand carries the service tool; right hand free.
Image 2 is the approved project's drawing STYLE ONLY: confident outer ink contours, secondary structure lines, restrained cel shadows, warm ivory ground. Do not take its aircraft identity, slenderness, wings, red-white palette, crown or weapons.

DESIGN THESIS: an old city repair machine can keep three generations of incompatible electric systems running at once. Make this visible as ONE coherent machine, with three signature features: a protected work-helmet head; an accessible equipment/cockpit torso with rounded stamped shells and joint gaiters; a compact rear power-distribution rack with a big cable reel and three visibly different adapter sockets. Wear shows maintenance and use, not catastrophic damage.

MAIN HEAD A: a properly tall three-dimensional teal industrial helmet with a short thick forward safety brow / ivory protective brow rail. Clear two separated amber camera eyes under the brow, small rectangular optical housings with a reassuring attentive expression. Ivory squared lower protective jaw, small practical breathing-like vent slots, no human mouth, no continuous horizontal cyclops strip. Readable forehead-to-chin height and cranium depth. A low yellow service ridge, one short flexible communications aerial mounted on the ear service block, small guarded work lamps. No large ear discs, dome bowl, police crest, samurai crown, fins, high horns, Gundam V or compressed flat head. A practical but appealing face the viewer can remember.
BODY: industrial load path, compact rounded-square chest equipment shell with one visibly latched ivory access/cockpit hatch and dark small inspection window, two little amber work lamps. Shoulders are plain protective service hoods, not angular pauldrons. Exposed black rubber accordion gaiters at elbows and hip; a few credible visible hydraulic actuators. Recognizable long upper legs, robust shins, and grounded steel-toe working feet. Keep an adult upright sturdy body with a visible neck, no hunched neck, no giant head, no dwarf proportions, no exaggerated thin runway legs. Large continuous surfaces, very few purposeful seams, bolts at replaceable panel corners. The replacement teal left shin has the SAME mounting pattern as its yellow counterpart.
EQUIPMENT: rear rack rises only to shoulder height. One clearly mounted cable reel on the upper rear, three different generation connector modules below (round/rectangular/heavy fork type), cable guides and a few secured cables. One left waist power-coupler holster and small right waist toolbox. LEFT hand holds a modest compact hydraulic rescue tool with a clear C-shaped jaw / quick-change working head and a hose connected to the left forearm service port, not an enormous military hammer. Right hand relaxed and empty. No missiles, gun barrels, wings, shields or oversized fuel tanks.
PALETTE: construction ochre yellow as main shell, teal helmet and selected service modules and left replacement shin, warm ivory forearm/access panels, charcoal mechanics; amber light. Restrained red safety tabs only. NO full-body hazard-stripe wallpaper, fluorescent green, military camo, decorative gold trim, or new franchise logo.
STYLE: polished hand-drawn cel mecha production concept, clean graphic massing and excellent readability, slight material wear, rich mechanical character without micro-panel noise. Warm neutral paper background, no street scene.
