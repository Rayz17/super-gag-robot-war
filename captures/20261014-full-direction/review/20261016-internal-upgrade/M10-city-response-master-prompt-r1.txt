CURRENT STORY DESIGN BASIS: an ORANGE AND WARM IVORY municipal fire-bridge apparatus reverses into a loading yard, plants FOUR stabilizing legs, and unfolds a broad bridge/heat-shield deck from its back to protect a passage. It also supplies water curtains to cool overheated structures. It belongs to its home-city fire crew. Occupational rescue machine, not a knight with a ladder and gun.
DESIGN: a heavy low vehicle-like rescue body, with FOUR clearly distinct articulated stabilizer/positioning legs at four corners. Each leg has two robust pivot links, a hydraulic jack and a broad load-bearing foot, with small protected wheel hardware for low-speed reversing/travel. The legs spread from front/rear corners around a central longitudinal carrier chassis; not four humanoid legs on a human pelvis, not insect spines, and no fifth/sixth support leg. All FOUR feet are planted and their attachments can be visually traced. Adult industrial scale, no cute toy body.
The front body contains a clearly usable armored cab/control housing with a dark continuous observation windscreen. Above it a compact tall fire-helmet-like sensor head: orange protective crown, amber/cyan optical pair, ivory heat mask, short rear service antenna. Preserve head volume, no flattened or oversized face. The head gives the apparatus a memorable reliable firefighter character, without recreating another franchise.
BACK STRUCTURE: ONE broad heavy bridge/heat-shield deck carried lengthwise on a real upper-back pivot cradle behind the cab. The deck has nested/hinged load-bearing leaves, solid anti-slip walking surface, thick side beams and handrail/edge guards. A bridge surface, NOT an open ladder and NOT a wing. Two visible hydraulic lift rams connect to the supporting chassis, with credible clearance above the cab/head. In travel posture the folded deck lies compactly along the carrier's back, not a huge floating plank, and does not block the head.
FIRE EQUIPMENT: ONE pair of water/foam cylinders, cream shells with teal inspection bands and orange caps, on the anatomical LEFT rear side. On RIGHT rear a pump/equipment box and restrained hose reel. Clearly secured hoses to TWO compact articulated front service booms ending in water/foam nozzles. These are short useful manipulators, not humanoid fists or cannon arms. No rifles, missiles, tanks full of glowing fuel or sword. Use visible water nozzles, couplings, emergency light blocks and heat-resistant articulated panels.
COLOR: current story specifies ORANGE and warm IVORY. Use rescue orange, warm ivory, graphite mechanics, teal fluid/inspection windows, small red safety latches/amber lamps. Do not repeat the earlier red-white main scheme, police blue-gray or M06 yellow-teal full palette. Same project family by ink and cel shading, not identical machine silhouette.
COMPOSITION: one landscape source sheet. Left 60% a large complete front-left-three-quarter view of the apparatus in planted ready stance with its bridge folded; position the four support legs so all FOUR feet can be counted, far legs partly visible, no arbitrary occluded extra legs. Upper right a smaller rear-three-quarter view of the same apparatus showing tank/pump sides and bridge pivot cradle. Lower right a clear side-operation vignette of this same unit with FOUR stabilizers planted: a broad solid deck unfolds forward over a small damaged concrete passage, leaving a sheltered evacuation lane below, and the front nozzles spray a modest WATER CURTAIN beside the lane for cooling. Show grounded support points and hydraulic deck connection, no floating beam/bridge or energy magic. Two tiny anonymous human silhouettes may establish the protected passage scale; they are not new characters.
Style: polished cel mecha production concept, confident black outer contours, restrained secondary mechanics, clear large masses and consistent maintained materials, not photoreal and not overloaded with panel scratches. Plain off-white sheet background. No text, lettering, numbers, logos or watermarks. Keep all machine extremities in frame. This is a coherent fire/bridge occupational apparatus, visually distinct from the police Labor and the repair humanoid.
