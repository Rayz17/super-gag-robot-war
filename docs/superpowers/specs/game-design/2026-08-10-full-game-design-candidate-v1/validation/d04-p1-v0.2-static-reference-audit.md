## Delivered boundary

The isolated package is at [data/d04-p1-v0.2](../data/d04-p1-v0.2/README.md). It contains a 14×10 two-band bridge world, three allied test units, five deterministic enemies, three autonomous vehicles, one 16-action allowlist, exact 0.5-second ticks, independent objectives, all 20 clock references, 28 A/B/C/D clock-vector expansions, two four-window regression seeds, a save/log schema and canonical hashes.

The checker is [check_d04_p1_v0_2.py](../scripts/check_d04_p1_v0_2.py). It is a read-only declared-event replay interpreter: it checks that stored events reproduce stored ledgers and hashes, but it does not derive those events from route, rotation, action, reaction, ownership or AI rules. It does not create a Godot scene, playable UI, Build, chapter binding or acceptance receipt.

## Recomputed seed ledger

| Seed | Vehicles | Bridge | Sample | Pairwise tradeoff |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| `SEED_FRONT_BRACE` | 2/3 clear | stress 3, intact, clear | enemy exited, forward failure | preserves bridge integrity |
| `SEED_SIDE_DIVERT` | 3/3 clear | stress 4, degraded, forward failure | ally custody, clear | saves the third vehicle and sample |

Brace only cancels positive stress created in the current window; it never repairs old stress. The front seed spends one engineering tool to repair the initial stress `1→0` before the first close. Both seeds then reproduce their four cycle stress ledgers exactly.

These two registered outcomes do not dominate one another. This is only a pairwise assertion over authored traces. A real common resolver must first prove that both traces are legal, and a bounded search must then test mixed plans such as repair plus intercept or opening the gate in a later window. Therefore no claim of two executable or globally valid strategies is made.

3. Put the same resolver behind four isolated Candidate Scheduler Builds without changing physics, AI, objectives or information.
