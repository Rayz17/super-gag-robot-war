## Reference contract

Use the two supplied approved GF full-screen references only for their restrained navy/cyan/white color relationship, hierarchy, finish quality, and the way tactical information yields to the scene. ## Generation prompt

Create one polished, original 16:9 landscape **standalone visual-direction board** for the dialogue interface of a contemporary Japanese mecha tactical command game. It should feel like a fusion of a colossal transforming robot museum's modern bridge avionics and an advanced public museum guidance system: precise, calm, high-information, thin, engineered, and unmistakably current.

Show six clearly separated interface-design specimens on a neutral graphite/navy presentation background, with generous dark gutters between them. They are concept specimens, not a full game screenshot:

1. A dominant wide dialogue module: smoked translucent navy/frosted composite panel, asymmetrical open construction, one structural side rail, a few short clipped planes, and at least two visually open edges. It must remain readable over a battlefield without behaving like a decorative picture frame.
2. A compact speaker/data tab: thin offset plate and one tiny status light, suitable for a modern sans-serif Chinese speaker name later, but contain no actual words.
3. A left-side local-scene/action-slice docking bracket: an open L-shaped or offset bracket that can anchor a cockpit or guide-seat scene insert without surrounding it with a complete frame.
4. A beat-progress element using a short horizontal rail, calibrated ticks, and flat segmented markers in idle/completed/current states. No gemstones, diamonds, badges, locks, or fantasy arrows.
5. A compact control-hint capsule family in idle/focus/pressed states: low-profile industrial control surfaces with cool-white and cyan response, no key letters or controller logos.
6. A restrained signal-divider/accent system: narrow cyan-white data pulse plus a tiny safety-orange decision cue, designed as short functional marks rather than ornament.

Visual language:

- Contemporary Japanese mecha tactical-command UI, modern aerospace avionics, precision industrial instrumentation, premium high-tech museum wayfinding.
- Asymmetrical, open, modular, edge-anchored; thin layers and precise clipped planes.
- Ordinary strokes should visually read as 1–3 px at 1280×720; main structural rails no more than 6–8 px; corner pieces compact, no larger than about 24×24 px at runtime scale.
- Material balance: about 75–80% graphite/deep navy, 14–18% cool white, no more than 3% signal cyan, no more than 2% safety amber/orange. Orange only marks a current decision or identity state, never a full outline.
- Smoked optical glass, matte graphite, thin anodized composite, subtle anti-glare frost, crisp controlled highlights. Low decoration, high information density.
- Clear hierarchy at 50% scale: dialogue field first, speaker second, beat progress and advance control third, secondary status last.
- The impression should be a light information layer extending from the screen edge, not an object placed on top of the scene.

Hard bans — any one of these makes the direction fail:

- No gold ornamental edging, gold filigree, engraved borders, or merely replacing gold with silver/cyan.
- No closed symmetric double/triple frame, no mirrored four-corner decoration, no thick D&D/RPG nine-slice frame.
- No gem diamonds, coat-of-arms, shields, lock emblems, runes, fantasy arrows, or equipment-rarity decoration.
- No Gothic, medieval, magical, steampunk, leather, stone, forged-metal, jewelry, embossed-relief, or ornate ceremonial styling.
- No oversized neon, particle magic, infinite scan-line spectacle, holographic clutter, dense unreadable microtext, or decorations louder than the dialogue.
- No characters, portraits, robots, maps, scenery, readable words, letters, numbers, logos, brand marks, watermarks, keyboard glyphs, or controller symbols.

Presentation requirements:

- Neutral dark graphite/navy board background is intentional for this style gate; native alpha is **not required for this direction-board call**.
- Six specimens must be spatially separate and visually inspectable at 50% scale.
- Clean professional game-UI concept board, front orthographic presentation, no perspective mockup, no phone/monitor frame, no explanatory labels.
- High fidelity, crisp silhouette, coherent material system, restrained glow, production-grade Japanese mecha game art direction.

This call succeeds only as a **modern style-direction Candidate** if, at 50% scale, it reads immediately as bridge avionics / museum guidance rather than fantasy RPG decoration. Even if successful, it does not authorize alpha extraction, component slicing, Godot use, Runtime, Default, Save, or Canon.
