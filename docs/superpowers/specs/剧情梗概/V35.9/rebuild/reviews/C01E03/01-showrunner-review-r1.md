## Findings

### [P1] The opening does not yet causally trap the five people

**Evidence:** The locked input requires exactly five people to begin inside (`C01E03-input-carry-v1.md`, §1, L11-L14). In the episode, however, the bridge driver has already reported stable pressure three times, and all five are said to have a route to the west gate (`可读剧情梗概` §1, L71-L75). Nobody starts a staggered withdrawal before Baihan arrives; the next physical cause is simply the four heavy latches overhead (L77-L81). Marking three necessary devices on the map explains what they want to bring, but it does not explain why all five people remain exposed after three stable reports.

This leaves the first dramatic necessity one question short: if each person already has an exit, why does the episode happen before they use it? As written, Baihan catches people who appear to have waited for the plot rather than people whom its action actively cuts off.

**Minimum repair:** In §1, make “stable” explicitly conditional on occupied support/pump posts, begin a real staggered handoff or first crossing immediately, and let Baihan's landing, load shift, or main-frame insertion physically interrupt that movement before the five can clear. One short action chain is enough. Preserve the exact five-person start, the necessary equipment, the 29 people outside, and people-first priority; do not add another accident or another trapped group.

### [P1] Jicun's defining choice exists in Story Core but is nearly invisible in the episode

**Evidence:** Jicun's personal desire is specifically not to save people with another answer “only he knows” (`Story Core`, L17-L23), and the effective temptation is a near-certain route that would make him the sole future controller while placing the retreat in Guardian Z's load zone (L31-L35). In the readable story, the offer itself is never staged. The audience only receives the retrospective negative sentence that he “did not call up” that route before sharing the maintenance track (`可读剧情梗概` §5, L135-L137). His later arm-entry, professional elimination, verification, and limited memory assist are strong active work (L161-L173), but they do not dramatize the earlier refusal.

The result is that Doudeng owns the visible irreversible decision, Haosi owns the visible forbidden action, while Jicun's episode-level inner turn remains packet metadata. A protagonist decision cannot rely on the reader imagining a menu he declined to open.

**Minimum repair:** At the shoulder-sink or battlefield-cutting beat, let the exclusive route recognize Jicun and visibly offer the high-success full-control action together with its five-person load-zone cost. Have him actively withdraw/break that control and convert the problem into four named public checks before exposing the maintenance track. Keep it brief and physical, without a theme speech. Preserve Doudeng as the sole chooser of the final cut, and preserve the memory fragment as only the fourth structural clue.

### [P1] The six military anchors compel E04 physically, but not yet causally

**Evidence:** The promised handoff is excellent in state terms: Guardian Z is single-armed, Brave Fist is being recovered, Baihan has learned, and the museum must move or be dismantled (`Story Core`, L53-L55). The readable ending then introduces six perfectly placed military anchors and a demolition countdown only after the battle and aftercare are over (`可读剧情梗概` §12, L229-L239). No earlier beat in E03 shows what observation, threshold, transmission, or opponent decision makes the military act at this exact moment.

The old engineering-map mystery should remain unanswered, but the action still needs a present-tense trigger. Without one, E04 begins because the next required state drops from outside the episode rather than because E03 forced an opponent to escalate.

**Minimum repair:** Seed one concrete trigger during the fight: for example, Guardian Z's first controlled displacement crosses an external immobilization threshold, or Baihan's withdrawal packet causes an already present military anchor array to align. At the tag, complete the six strikes because that visible trigger has fired. Do not explain who supplied the old plan, add a new command-room scene, or soften the physical lock.

### [P2] Baihan's learning briefly falls back into design-memo language

**Evidence:** Baihan already demonstrates adaptive intelligence through action when it turns its bent frame into a new ground support (`可读剧情梗概` §6, L147-L151). The retreat then restates the lesson as a three-item abstract log, followed by Jicun verbalizing the general rule that every elegant solution teaches the opponent (§10, L195-L201). This duplicates a meaning the body action has already made legible and echoes E02's “enemy acquired a sample” settlement grammar.

**Minimum repair:** Keep the required sample and Jicun's alarm, but embody the extraction in one observable act: Baihan tracks the arm-to-bridge trajectory, re-indexes one damaged frame movement, or departs using a partial imitation. Reduce the three-item thesis list to at most one incomplete tactical datum. No state or Canon change is needed.
